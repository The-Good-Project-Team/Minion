Installing Minion 2

Your private memory, upgraded. Takes about two minutes of your time.

Minion 2 brings everything from your current Minion over automatically — you won't lose a single note. Just follow these steps in order.

1. Put Minion 2 in your Applications

  1. You've already opened this folder, so you can see Minion 2 sitting next to this guide.
  2. Drag Minion 2 into your Applications folder.
  3. Double-click it to open.

If macOS says "Minion 2 can't be opened because Apple cannot check it": right-click the app, choose Open, then click Open again. You only do this once.

2. Let it bring your memory over

The first time it opens, Minion 2 copies everything from your old Minion by itself. There is nothing for you to click.

  • Your notes are searchable within seconds.
  • The smarter, deeper search keeps sharpening quietly in the background for up to an hour. You can use the app the whole time.
  • Your old Minion is left completely untouched, just in case.

3. Check that Claude can see your notes

Minion 2 reconnects Claude to your memory automatically. Open Claude and ask it something only your notes would know. When it answers, you're all set.

If Claude comes up empty, fully quit Claude (Cmd + Q) and reopen it.

Important last step. Once Claude is working with Minion 2, please remove the old Minion — otherwise the two versions quietly fight over Claude. It only takes a moment (next page... er, next section).

4. Remove the old Minion

  1. In the menu bar at the top of your screen, find the old Minion icon, click it, and choose Quit.
  2. Open System Settings → General → Login Items, select Minion in the list, and click the minus (–) button so it stops starting up on its own.
  3. Open your Applications folder, find the old Minion (the one without the "2"), and drag it to the Trash.

That's it. Minion 2 is now your memory, and Claude is pointed at it.

If something looks off

  • Search feels thin at first — that's normal in the first hour while the deeper search finishes building. Keyword search works right away.
  • Claude isn't finding anything — quit Claude fully (Cmd + Q) and reopen it.
  • You're ever unsure — your old Minion still has all your original data and still works. Nothing was deleted. You can always fall back to it.
